## Current Focus

## Key Decisions
- **Parser Library:** `ruamel.yaml` selected over `PyYAML` to support comments preservation, line/column tracking, and YAML 1.2 compliance.
- **Mixed Style Management:** Track sequence indentation styles (Compact vs. Nested) dynamically during stack analysis. Offer safe shift operations to rewrite files uniformly.
